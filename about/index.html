Maplewing Press is an independent publisher of thoughtfully made books. Our first series is Maplewing Dreams, coloring stories for older adults.

Maplewing was started by Tracy Kondracki, a registered nurse who ran an adult family home for 18 years. Coloring was a favorite pastime there, and she noticed something: the same picture could delight one person and overwhelm another. Eyesight and memory made all the difference, and a page that worked last year might feel too busy this year. Maplewing Dreams grew from that. Each story comes in three levels, so everyone can enjoy the same pictures in the way that suits them.

Our name comes from the samara, the little winged seed that spins down from the red maple each spring. Like a samara, our stories are small things meant to travel: from a book to a kitchen table or a care home activity room.
