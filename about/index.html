Maplewing Press is a small, family-run publisher of coloring stories and storybook readers.

Our name comes from the maple samara, the winged seed that spins down from maple trees each autumn. Like a samara, our stories are small things meant to travel: from a book to a kitchen table, a care home activity room or a homeschool shelf.
