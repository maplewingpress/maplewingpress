Maplewing Press is an independent publisher of thoughtfully made books. Our first series is Maplewing Dreams, coloring books for older adults.

Our name comes from the maple samara, the winged seed that spins down from maple trees each autumn. Like a samara, our stories are small things meant to travel: from a book to a kitchen table or a care home activity room.
