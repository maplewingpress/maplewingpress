Which level is right?

  • EasyThe whole scene, with plenty of little details to discover.
  • EasierA closer view with bigger shapes and less background. Large print.
  • EasiestUp close and simple. Big shapes, clean lines, almost no background. Large print.

Pick the level that feels comfortable. It's about ease, not ability.
