Using our pages

Printable pages included with a book are for one household, or private caregiving for up to four people. Facilities need a facility license. Please don't share the files or post them online.

Privacy

This website does not use tracking cookies. If you send us your Amazon order number, we use it only to confirm your book and send your pages. If you join our mailing list, you can leave at any time from any email.
