Activity directors can use Maplewing Dreams with every resident, at whatever level suits them, from one license.

What a facility license includes

  • All three levels of a book (Easy, Easier and Easiest) plus the printable pages that come with them.
  • Printing for residents at one location.
  • Small facility (5 to 20 licensed beds) and large facility (21 beds or more) options.
