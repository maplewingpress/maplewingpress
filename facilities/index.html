One story, three levels, and every resident can join in. A facility license lets your team print Easy, Easier and Easiest pages for everyone at your location.

What a facility license includes

  • All three levels of a book (Easy, Easier and Easiest) plus the printable pages that come with them.
  • Printing for residents at one location.
  • Small facility (5 to 20 licensed beds) and large facility (21 beds or more) options.

Created by a registered nurse who ran an adult family home for 18 years, with care settings in mind.
