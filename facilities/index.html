  1. Choose your book and buy the license with your facility name and email.

Questions

What counts as one location?
One building or home at one street address. An adult family home counts as one location.
We have more than one location.
Email {{ site.facilities_email }} and we'll work out a fair price with you.
Can families take pages home?
Pages are for residents, clients and activities at your location. Families who'd like their own copy can buy the book.
Does the license include future books?
For now, each license covers one book. As our collection of coloring books for older adults grows, we plan to offer a collection license that covers them all. Do I need a license if I bought the book?
A book is for personal or family use. To print pages for a group or a care setting, choose a facility license.
Why is it less if we own the book?
You've already bought the story once, so you pay $36. Enter your order number on your book's printable pages to get the book-owner price.
Why do you need our email?
It's where we send your license certificate and download link, so you can come back and print again anytime. We'll also send license holders a short note when a new book or the collection license is ready, never the monthly newsletter unless you join it. You can unsubscribe anytime.
Can we get an invoice or W-9?
Yes. Email {{ site.facilities_email }}.
