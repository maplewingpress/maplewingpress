About the books

Who are these books for?
Older adults who enjoy coloring, including people living with dementia, Alzheimer's or low vision, and the family members, caregivers and activity teams who color with them.
Are they children's books?
No. Children may enjoy coloring them too, but every Maplewing Dreams book is designed with older adults in mind. The art is realistic and gentle, never cartoonish. These are grown-up books that respect the person holding them.
Which level should I choose?
Easy shows the whole scene with lots of detail. Easier moves in closer, with bigger shapes and less background. Easiest is up close, simple and bold, with almost no background. All three levels are large print, and Easier and Easiest are bold too. If you're unsure, start with Easier. You can compare the levels side by side.
What size are the pages?
8.5 by 11 inches, 51 single-sided pages per book.
Where can I buy them?
On Amazon. The Halloween book is available on Amazon soon.

Printable pages

What are the printable pages?
Every book includes printable copies of all its pages. You can print a favorite and color it again, as often as you like at home.
How do I get them?
Scan the QR code inside your book, or choose your book on our Printable Pages list. Tell us your Amazon order number, and the download link appears on the screen.
Do I have to join the mailing list?
No. You never need to sign up to get your book's printable pages.
Who can use them?
Printable pages included with a book are for one household, or private caregiving for up to four people. Facilities need a facility license. Please don't share the files or post them online.
Does my book include the other levels?
No. Your printable pages are for the level you bought. Want all three levels at home? The Family Set has every page in Easy, Easier and Easiest. It's $24, or $15 if you own the book. A facility license also includes all three levels.
What is the Family Set?
All three levels of one book as printable pages, for your household, including a loved one you care for at home. Each person can choose a level, every time you print. Book owners find the $15 code on their book's printable pages. See the Family Set.
What paper and printer should I use?
Everyday copy paper works well for colored pencils. For markers, choose smooth, heavier paper, such as cardstock. See our printing and coloring tips.

Our mailing list

What do I get?
Three extra bonus pages that aren't in the book, in all three levels. They arrive in your welcome email.
How many emails will I get?
We keep it light. Expect a welcome email, one follow-up, then about one email a month with a new coloring page and a note when the next story comes out.
Can I leave?
Yes. You can leave at any time from any email.

For facilities

Do I need a license if I bought the book?
A book is for personal or family use. To print pages for a group or a care setting, choose a facility license.
What does a facility license cost?
$45 per book, with all three levels. If you already own the book, it's $37. Find the code on your book's printable pages. See facility licenses.
What counts as one location?
One building or home at one street address. An adult family home counts as one location.

Yes. Email {{ site.facilities_email }}.
