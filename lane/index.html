Maplewing Lane brings the same family and neighborhood to young readers, with readers built on systematic phonics and activities that connect reading to nature, history and math.

The first titles are in the works. Topics planned include Halloween, monarch butterflies, Christmas, puppies, Route 66, and owls and woodpeckers.
