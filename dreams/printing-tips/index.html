Your book comes with printable copies of every page. Here's how to get beautiful prints at home.

Find your printer

  • Laser printer: Great for every kind of marker. The lines stay crisp.
  • Inkjet printer: Great with colored pencils and crayons. For markers, choose alcohol markers. Give the print a few minutes to dry first.
  • Not sure which you have? Inkjet printers use liquid ink cartridges. Laser printers use toner. A local print shop or library can print on a laser printer for you.

Pick your paper

  • Colored pencils and crayons: Everyday copy paper works well. Paper with a little texture holds more color.
  • Markers: Choose smooth, heavier paper, such as cardstock. Colors stay bright and clean.
  • Watercolors: Choose watercolor paper that fits your printer. Look for "inkjet watercolor paper" or "mixed media" paper. Print on a laser printer if you can.
  • Bigger lines for tired eyes: Print at 100% or "actual size". The pages are made to fill a letter-size sheet.

Try other dry media

Colored pencils are just the start. Each of these brings its own look.

  • Oil pastels: Rich, creamy color, bolder than crayons. No dust.
  • Pastel pencils: Soft pastel color in pencil form. Tidy and easy to control.
  • Wax pastels and gel crayons: Smooth, bright color that glides right on.
  • Watercolor pencils and crayons: Use them dry, or touch them with a damp brush for a painted look. Print on cardstock first.
  • Soft (chalk) pastels: The most vibrant and blendable of all. Work over a tray, and keep a damp cloth nearby.
  • Charcoal: Lovely blacks and grays for shading.

Our favorites for easy coloring: Oil pastels, pastel pencils, gel crayons and watercolor pencils. They give bright color with very little mess.

Helpful habits

  • Print a fresh copy anytime you'd like to try new colors.
  • Keep a backing sheet under your page when you use markers.
  • Try your colors on the swatch page at the front of your book.
