A coloring page can be a quiet, happy half hour together. These simple tips come from 18 years of coloring with the residents of an adult family home.

Set up for success

  • Pick a level that feels easy today. You can always move up or down.
  • Set out just a few colors to start. Too many choices can feel like work.
  • Use good light, and a table at a comfortable height.
  • Colored pencils with a thick barrel are easier to hold.

Make it a visit, not a task

  • Sit side by side and color your own page too.
  • Talk about the picture. "Did you ever pick pumpkins?" "What color was your first car?"
  • Soft music they love can set a cozy mood.
  • Praise the colors and the effort, not how neatly it's done.

Let them lead

  • There's no wrong way to color. Purple pumpkins are welcome.
  • It's fine to stop halfway. A page can wait for another day.
  • If a page feels too busy, try the same picture at an easier level.

Keep the joy going

  • Use your printing rights to print a favorite page again. See our printing and coloring tips.
  • Hang a finished page on the fridge, or mail it to a grandchild.

Maplewing Dreams was created by Tracy Kondracki, RN, who ran an adult family home for 18 years. Every story comes in three levels, so everyone at the table can color the same pictures.
