Pour a cup of tea and settle in. Maplewing Dreams coloring books have a gentle, nostalgic storybook feel. The pictures are realistic, never childish cartoons. Every book is made with older adults in mind.

Each book tells a quiet story without words. Every page is a lovely picture all by itself. Turn the pages in order, and you may notice the story unfolding. Keep an eye out for familiar faces from book to book. The art has clean black outlines and no shading. Pages are printed on one side, so each picture has a plain back.

Why three levels?

Maplewing Dreams was created by a registered nurse who ran an adult family home for 18 years. She saw how differently each person enjoyed a picture, depending on their eyesight and memory, and how that could change over time. So every story comes in three levels of detail. Families and care teams can choose the one that fits today, and switch when needs change.

Questions

Who are these books for?
Older adults who enjoy coloring, including people living with dementia, Alzheimer's or low vision, and the family members, caregivers and activity teams who color with them.
Are they children's books?
No. Children may enjoy coloring them too, but every Maplewing Dreams book is designed with older adults in mind. The art is realistic and gentle, never cartoonish. These are grown-up books that respect the person holding them.
What size are the pages?
8.5 by 11 inches, 51 single-sided pages per book.
Which level should I choose?
Easy shows the whole scene with lots of detail. Easier moves in closer, with bigger shapes and less background. Easiest is up close, simple and bold, with almost no background. All three levels are large print, and Easier and Easiest are bold too. If you're unsure, start with Easier.
