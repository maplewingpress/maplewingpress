--- layout: page section: dreams title: Maplewing Dreams eyebrow: Coloring stories for older adults description: Coloring stories for older adults in a classic 1950s storybook style, in Easy, Easier and Easiest levels. ---

Each Maplewing Dreams book follows one family through a season in a small Illinois town: Meg and Max, baby Tess, Mom and Dad, Nana Mae, Daisy the golden retriever and Kip the cat.

The pictures are drawn in a realistic, classic storybook style with clean black outlines and no shading, printed on one side of the page so markers won't bleed through.
